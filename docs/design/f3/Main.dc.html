Location is blocked

We can’t verify a deed without your location. You can turn it back on in a few taps.

How to turn it on

  1. 1Tap the icon to the left of the web address (a lock or settings icon).
  2. 2Find Location and choose Allow.
  3. 3Come back here and tap Try again.

Still not working? Check that Location is switched on in your phone’s settings.
